🔑 Admin cheats
Move ideas cheat sheet
Opening: take the center (e4/d4), develop knights then bishops, castle by move 8, don’t move a piece twice, don’t rush the queen out.
Every move, check: 1) their checks, 2) their captures, 3) their threats — then yours. Never leave a piece undefended.
Tactics: forks (knights and pawns), pins, skewers, discovered attacks, back-rank mate. Trade pieces when you’re ahead.
Middlegame: rooks on open files, knights on strong central squares, bishops on long diagonals, attack weak pawns.
Endgame: activate your king, push passed pawns, keep rooks behind them.
Vs the bot: Easy hangs pieces — look for free captures; Hard punishes blunders.
